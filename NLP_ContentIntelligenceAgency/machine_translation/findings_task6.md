# Translation Model Findings

The errors in the translation model can be categorized into a few key types: tense errors, where the model incorrectly translates verb conjugations (missing past or future tense markers); negation errors, where the model misinterprets or omits negations, leading to opposite meanings; and structural errors, where word order or expressions do not align with natural English language.

From a linguistic standpoint, these mistakes are explainable. French and English have different grammatical structures—negation in French often involves "ne...pas," which might be misinterpreted, and verb tenses in French do not always have a one-to-one mapping to English. Additionally, there are some expressions that rarely translate literally, causing unnatural sentences, and this might get to affect the model negatively.

With an accuracy of 93.7%, the model performs exceptionally well. This means the majority of translations are correct or require only minor adjustments.
